Privacy Policy

How Tanzania Safari Magic handles your information.

Last updated: August 2026

Tanzania Safari Magic (“we”, “us”) operates tanzaniasafarimagic.com from Arusha, Tanzania. This policy explains what we collect when you browse, enquire, or request a safari quote.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details you submit (name, email, phone, country, travel dates, messages)
  • Booking/enquiry details and preferred packages
  • Technical data such as pages visited, referrer, approximate device/browser (first-party analytics; IP addresses are hashed)
  • Communications via WhatsApp, email, or live chat

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to quotes, bookings, and support requests
  • To prepare safari itineraries and coordinate with lodges/guides
  • To improve our website and understand which pages are useful
  • To send trip-related updates you have requested (not spam marketing lists without consent)

3. Sharing

We may share necessary trip details with trusted suppliers (lodges, parks authorities processes, transfer partners) solely to deliver your safari. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Payments

Online card checkout may be enabled in the future. Today, deposits and balances are typically arranged offline (bank transfer / agreed methods) after Our Team confirms your itinerary. Payment confirmations are recorded in our booking system.

5. Cookies & analytics

We use essential cookies/local storage for site function (e.g. chat session, analytics visitor id). You can clear browser storage anytime. See our cookie notice on first visit.

6. Retention & security

We keep enquiry and booking records as needed for operations and legal obligations. Access to admin systems is restricted to authorized staff.

7. Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data we hold, subject to legal retention needs. Contact us using the details below.
