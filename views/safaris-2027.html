Why this itinerary

12 days. Wildlife, culture, and the migration.

Discover the diverse beauty of Tanzania on a safari that combines wildlife, nature, authentic culture and the Great Migration. You begin in Western Kilimanjaro, exploring the less-visited Sinya wildlife area, then continue to Tarangire for baobabs and elephant herds, Lake Eyasi for a Hadzabe hunting encounter, and the legendary Serengeti — central first, then three days in the north during September.

The journey ends in the Ngorongoro Highlands with a full-day crater safari, searching for the Big Five including black rhino, before your transfer back to Kilimanjaro Airport.

Frequently asked questions

When is this 2027 safari?

2 September to 13 September 2027. It is a 12-day / 11-night itinerary starting and ending at Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO).

How much does it cost?

The published price is $5,913 per person. Ask us about single-room supplements, child rates, or a private vehicle upgrade.

Will I see a Mara River crossing?

September is a strong month for northern Serengeti herds, but crossings are wild events — never guaranteed. Three nights in the north give you more time to follow the animals if they are in the area.

Is this a group safari or private?

This is a scheduled 2027 departure. Write to us with your party size: couples, families and small groups can often travel on the same dates with a private vehicle if you prefer.

Are international flights included?

No. You fly into Kilimanjaro (JRO) on 2 September 2027 (or the evening before) and depart from JRO on 13 September. We handle all ground transfers once you land.

Do I need a visa and vaccinations?

Most visitors need a Tanzania e-visa. Yellow fever proof may be required if you arrive from a risk country; malaria prophylaxis is recommended. We send a practical checklist when you book.

Can I add Zanzibar after this safari?

Yes. This itinerary ends in Arusha / JRO on 13 September. Beach nights belong on our dedicated Zanzibar collection — we can attach a flight and lodge after day 12.

Tell us your name, party size and flight plans. Our Team in Arusha replies, usually within a few hours on WhatsApp.
