How Much Will Your Tanzania Safari Cost?
Tanzania is not the cheapest safari country in Africa — and that is partly why parks feel less crowded and wildlife experiences stay premium. You can still safari well on a controlled budget if you book locally, travel in shoulder seasons, and choose lodges wisely.
A realistic planning range for a classic private northern circuit (excluding international flights) often falls between 
$2,000 and $8,000+ per person
 for 5–10 days, depending on comfort and season.

Factors Affecting Your Tanzania Safari Cost
  
Season
 — Peak dry (June–Oct) and calving (Jan–Feb) cost more; long rains can mean deals
  
Lodge tier
 — Inside-park nights cost more than stays outside gates
  
Group size
 — Private safaris for 2 cost more pp than 4–6 sharing one vehicle
  
Transport
 — Fly-in segments raise comfort and price vs road circuits
  
Park mix
 — Serengeti &amp; Ngorongoro fees are higher than some southern parks
  
Who you book with
 — Local operator vs overseas agent markup

Tanzania Safari Cost FAQs
  
Is $350 per person per day realistic for a private safari?
Yes for a lean private budget safari with a local operator — simpler lodges, efficient routing, and fewer luxury extras. Mid-range comfort usually sits higher.
  
Why are Tanzania safaris more expensive than some other countries?
Park fees, logistics across large distances, and strong demand for Serengeti/Ngorongoro raise costs — while helping fund conservation and keeping visitor density lower in many areas.
  
Do your package prices include park fees?
Most Tanzania Safari Magic quotes include park fees, lodging, guiding, and safari meals as listed. Always confirm the inclusion list on your proposal.
