The Role of the Rains
Tanzania’s safari calendar is driven by rainfall more than a single “high season” stamp. Short rains (often late October–December) and long rains (often March–May) green the plains, refill waterholes, and push wildebeest herds along their loop.
  
Dry months
 concentrate wildlife and favour classic photography
  
Calving rains window
 brings predators to southern short-grass plains
  
Long rains
 can close some camps and slow road circuits — still workable with local routing
  
Shoulders
 often balance good sightings with softer prices
Herd location is never guaranteed week-to-week. Deep migration planning belongs in our 
Great Wildebeest Migration guide
 and on the

Month-by-Month Safari Summaries
Compact snapshots for planning. Always confirm current herd reports and lodge openings with 
Our Team
 before you lock flights.
January
  
Pros:
 Calving drama building in the south; warm days; strong predator action
  
Cons:
 Possible short showers; popular with migration seekers
  
Wildlife / migration:
 Southern Serengeti &amp; Ndutu calving focus — see 
migration guide
  
Zanzibar note:
 Often excellent beach weather for a post-safari extension
February
  
Pros:
 Peak calving weeks; superb cat sightings on short grass
  
Cons:
 Hot afternoons; book southern camps early
  
Wildlife / migration:
 Still south-focused herds; classic Big Five circuits elsewhere
  
Zanzibar note:
 Strong swimming window before longer rains inland
March
  
Pros:
 Late calving / early herd shifts; greening landscapes; some value appearing
  
Cons:
 Long rains can start; itineraries need flexibility
  
Wildlife / migration:
 Herds begin leaving deep south; still rewarding with the right camps
  
Zanzibar note:
 Showers more likely — still fine for mixed indoor/outdoor days
April
  
Pros:
 Lush scenery; fewer vehicles; attractive lodge rates
  
Cons:
 Heavier rain risk; some remote camps close
  
Wildlife / migration:
 Dispersed herds; birdwatching excellent; resident game still strong in many parks
  
Zanzibar note:
 Wetter — better for travellers who accept rain between sun spells
May
  
Pros:
 Shoulder value; Western Corridor interest as herds move
  
Cons:
 Variable roads; confirm open lodges
  
Wildlife / migration:
 Grumeti / western moves often begin — detail in 
Serengeti guide
  
Zanzibar note:
 Improving toward drier months; ask for current beach forecasts
June
  
Pros:
 Dry season starts; clearer viewing; migration pushing west/north
  
Cons:
 Demand rising; cooler mornings
  
Wildlife / migration:
 Strong all-round safari month; western crossings possible
  
Zanzibar note:
 Popular beach start — book hotels early for July–August follow-ons
July
  
Pros:
 Peak dry viewing; Mara River crossing season typically opens
  
Cons:
 Highest crowds &amp; rates in northern Serengeti
  
Wildlife / migration:
 Northern Serengeti focus — pair with 
Ngorongoro
 for a classic circuit
  
Zanzibar note:
 Excellent beach add-on after crossing weeks
August
  
Pros:
 Iconic dry-season safari; strong crossing odds in good years
  
Cons:
 Peak pricing; popular camps sell out months ahead
  
Wildlife / migration:
 Northern herds &amp; predators; resident game superb park-wide
  
Zanzibar note:
 Busy but beautiful — secure beach nights with your safari quote
September
  
Pros:
 Still dry and productive; slightly less frantic than August in some areas
  
Cons:
 Dusty tracks; heat building toward October
  
Wildlife / migration:
 Crossings can continue; excellent general game
  
Zanzibar note:
 Reliable beach recovery month
October
  
Pros:
 Late dry concentrations; dramatic skies as short rains approach
  
Cons:
 Hot days; herds may begin shifting
  
Wildlife / migration:
 Late northern action then transitions; Tarangire elephants often superb
  
Zanzibar note:
 Still strong early; showers increase later — see 
Zanzibar guide
November
  
Pros:
 Fresh green flush; fewer peak-season crowds; better rates
  
Cons:
 Short rains; afternoon storms possible
  
Wildlife / migration:
 Herds often moving through central/south transitions; photography lush
  
Zanzibar note:
 Mixed — plan flexible beach activities
December
  
Pros:
 Festive travel energy; building toward calving; family-friendly packages
  
Cons:
 Holiday rate spikes; stormy spells
  
Wildlife / migration:
 Southern plains interest rises again toward year-end
  
Zanzibar note:
 Popular holiday beach demand — book early

Kilimanjaro Timing
Mount Kilimanjaro
 climbs favour clearer, drier windows — commonly 
January–March
 and 
June–October
. Wet months mean muddier trails and more cloud. We sequence trek days before or after safari so you are not rushing summit recovery into long game-drive mornings.
Pair Kili with a short northern circuit, then optional 
Zanzibar
 if you want beach recovery after the mountain.
Zanzibar Timing
Beach comfort is strongest in the same broad dry stretches that suit safari — especially 
June–October
 and 
January–February
. Greener months still work if you accept showers and choose hotels with indoor lounge space.
