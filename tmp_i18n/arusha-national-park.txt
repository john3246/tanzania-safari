About Arusha National Park
Often overlooked between airport arrivals and the drive west to Tarangire or Ngorongoro, 
Arusha National Park
 offers a different pace: ranger-escorted walks, canoe time on alkaline lakes, and forest game drives with Meru dominating the skyline.
I’m 
John Raphael Shayo
. With Our Team at Tanzania Safari Magic, we recommend this park when guests have a free afternoon after landing, want an active day before long game-drive circuits, or need a gentle introduction for families and first-time safari travellers.
  
  
Arusha town — your safari base, with the national park a short drive away.
Wildlife (Not a Classic Big Five Park)
Set expectations clearly: Arusha National Park is 
not
 where you go for lions, leopards, or rhinos on a standard day trip. What you 
do
 get is memorable:
  
Black-and-white colobus monkeys
 — a highlight in the forest canopy
  
Giraffe &amp; buffalo
 — often on the open “Little Serengeti” plains
  
Zebra, warthog, waterbuck
 — common on game drives
  
Birdlife
 — flamingos and waterbirds around Momella when conditions favour them; forest species inland
  
Hippo
 — occasional lake-edge sightings
Save Big Five intensity for 
Serengeti
 and 
Ngorongoro Crater
 days on the same holiday.
Climate
Elevation keeps mornings cooler than the lowland parks. Forest shade helps on walks; lake shores can be bright and reflective at midday. Long rains make some trails muddier — still workable with the right footwear and flexible timing.

Best Time to Visit Arusha National Park
June–October

Park Areas to Know
  
Little Serengeti (Serengeti Ndogo)
 — open grassland for giraffe and buffalo views with Meru backdrop
  
Ngurdoto Crater
 — forested caldera viewpoint (vehicles stay on the rim; no floor descent for guests)
  
Momella Lakes
 — alkaline lakes for canoeing, birds, and reflective mountain photos
  
Waterfalls &amp; forest trails
 — guided walks to falls and colobus habitat (ranger required)

Activities: Walk, Canoe &amp; Meru
  
Guided walking safari
 — ranger-escorted forest and waterfall trails; a rare active contrast to vehicle-only parks
  
Canoeing on Momella Lakes

Game drives
 — pop-up 4x4 loops through plains and forest edges
  
Mount Meru climbing

Worth It as a Safari Add-On?
Yes
 when you value walking, canoeing, forest primates, and Meru scenery — or when you have a spare day in Arusha before heading to 
Serengeti
, 
Ngorongoro
, or 
Tarangire
.
Skip or shorten
 if every day must maximise Big Five sightings and your itinerary is already tight. In that case, go straight west and keep Arusha as a sleep-and-depart base only.
Where to Stay in Arusha Town
Almost everyone bases in 
Arusha town or nearby coffee-lodge country
 rather than inside the park. Choose a hotel that matches your arrival: airport transfer friendly, early-breakfast capable for safari departure days, and quiet enough after long flights.
